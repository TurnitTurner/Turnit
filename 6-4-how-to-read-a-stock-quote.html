Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Ticker, price, change, volume, and market cap are the fields you already knew.
✓A stock can open above or below yesterday's close — that difference is called a gap.
✓Day's Range is today's swing. 52-Week Range is the whole year's.
✓The spread is simply the ask minus the bid — a narrower spread usually means a more liquid stock.
✓Dividend yield is the annual dividend divided by the share price.
✓A full quote is just all of these fields, sitting on one screen.
