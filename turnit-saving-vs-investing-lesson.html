Myth or Fact · 7 / 10

Myth or fact?

Three beliefs that trip up beginners before they even start. Tap your answer for each.

Keeping all your money in savings is the "safe" choice long-term.

Myth. It feels safe day-to-day, but inflation quietly erodes cash that isn't growing fast enough over many years.

You need a large emergency fund before you can start investing at all.

Myth, mostly. Many people build a small starter cushion first, then invest and grow savings side by side rather than waiting for a huge fund.

Money you'll need within the next couple of years generally belongs in savings, not investments.

Fact. Short-term money can't afford a bad-timing dip right before you need it — that's exactly what savings is for.
