Recap · 12 / 13

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Without an emergency fund, a surprise cost forces you into high-interest debt or selling investments at a bad time.
✓Your financial runway is your cash divided by your monthly expenses — aim for 3-6 months.
✓A real emergency fund covers job loss, medical bills, major repairs, and family emergencies — not discretionary wants.
✓Keep it in a high-yield savings account — not checking, and not the stock market.
✓Build it in phases: a $1,000 starter core first, then the full 3-6 month shield.
✓A cash shield protects your investments from being sold off — or your debt from piling up — during a crisis.
Your next move: calculate your own monthly runway, and automate a transfer toward your starter core.
