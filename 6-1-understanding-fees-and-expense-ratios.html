Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Expense ratios, trading commissions, account fees, and advisor fees are all different things.
✓Some fees arrive as a bill you can read. Others are quietly subtracted before you ever see a number.
✓The same fee percentage costs more real dollars every year as your balance grows.
✓Load fees are sales charges taken at the door — either going in or coming out.
✓Funds tracking the exact same strategy can still charge very different fees.
✓Checking a fund's expense ratio before buying takes seconds and can matter for decades.
Your next move: look up the expense ratio on anything you already own — you may be surprised what you find.
