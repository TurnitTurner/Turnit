Recap · 9 / 10

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓A market order guarantees speed. A limit order guarantees price. Neither guarantees both.
✓Slippage is the gap between the price you saw and the price you actually got.
✓A limit order set too conservatively can simply never fill.
✓A stop order stays dormant until the price actually crosses your trigger.
✓A Day Order expires at market close. A GTC order sticks around until filled or cancelled.
✓Choosing an order type is really choosing what you're willing to leave uncertain.
Your next move: next time you place a trade, actually think about which one you're giving up — speed or price.
