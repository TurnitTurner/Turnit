Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓A gain is just "on paper" and untaxed until you actually sell — that's the realized vs. unrealized line.
✓A capital loss can offset a capital gain, lowering what you actually owe.
✓Short-term and long-term trades are tallied separately, then combined into one final number.
✓Buying back a security too soon after a loss can trigger the wash sale rule and disqualify it.
✓None of this applies inside a Roth or Traditional IRA — that's one of their core advantages.
✓This is genuinely complex territory — a tax professional is worth it once real money is involved.
Your next move: if you've ever sold an investment at a loss, check whether you actually claimed it.
