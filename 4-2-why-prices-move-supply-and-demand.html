Recap · 9 / 10

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Every price is a live tug-of-war between buyers and sellers — nothing more mystical than that.
✓Earnings, leadership changes, interest rates, and product news all shift that balance.
✓Prices react to whether results beat or miss expectations — not just whether they're good.
✓Low trading volume can make the same trade swing a price much harder.
✓Fear and greed can push short-term prices further than the actual numbers justify.
✓Zoomed out, day-to-day noise tends to fade into the real, longer-term trend.
Your next move: next time a price jumps, ask what expectation just got beaten or missed.
