Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓If your employer offers a 401(k) match, that usually comes first — it's close to guaranteed free money.
✓Money you'll need soon shouldn't be locked into a retirement account — a brokerage keeps it accessible.
✓For long-term money, your current vs. expected future tax bracket points toward Roth or Traditional.
✓There's no single "right" account — the right one depends on your actual situation.
✓Most people eventually end up using more than one account type at once.
✓The best account is the one you actually open and use — not the theoretically perfect one you never start.
Your next move: use today's answers to pick one account, and carry it into the next lesson to actually open it.
