Recap · 12 / 13

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Every discretionary dollar is a choice between lifestyle spending and your future freedom fund.
✓Small recurring costs, like forgotten subscriptions, compound into serious money when redirected.
✓A real need passes the survival test, has no substitute, can't wait, and has no flashier "upgrade."
✓A 48-hour cooldown filters out most impulse buys without needing any willpower in the moment.
✓Saving even part of a raise, instead of spending all of it, keeps lifestyle creep from erasing your progress.
✓Automating your split means you don't have to rely on discipline every single time.
Your next move: cancel one subscription you don't actually use, and redirect it into your investing account.
