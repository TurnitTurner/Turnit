Recap · 9 / 10

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓A share is one slice of ownership out of however many total shares a company has issued.
✓Share price × shares outstanding = market cap, the company's total value.
✓Shareholders can get voting rights, sometimes dividends, and a claim on leftover assets.
✓Shares are first created and sold at an IPO, then trade between investors afterward.
✓The most you can ever lose owning a share is what you paid — never more.
✓Owning a share means owning a real piece of a real business — not just a number on a screen.
Your next move: next time a price ticks, remember it's a real business changing hands, one slice at a time.
