Recap · 9 / 10

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Spreading eggs across baskets means one drop doesn't break everything you own.
✓Real diversification stacks three layers: asset class, within-class, and geography.
✓Different countries and regions move on their own economic cycles.
✓Owning many similar things that move together isn't real diversification — correlation matters more than count.
✓Benefits shrink fast after a certain point — piling on more mostly adds complexity, not safety.
✓A single broad fund can often check most of these boxes in one purchase.
Your next move: look at what you already own and ask whether it's actually diversified, or just numerous.
