Recap · 9 / 10

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓A Roth taxes your contribution today. A Traditional taxes your withdrawal later. Someone always pays the toll.
✓Qualified Roth withdrawals in retirement come out with no tax owed on the growth.
✓A Traditional contribution lowers your taxable income in the same year you make it.
✓Both accounts hold the same kinds of investments — stocks, bonds, ETFs, and mutual funds.
✓Both are built to stay invested until retirement age, so pulling growth out early usually costs you.
✓The better choice usually comes down to your tax bracket now versus your best guess about retirement.
Your next move: write down your tax bracket today and your honest guess for retirement — then look at contribution limits and age rules in the next lesson.
