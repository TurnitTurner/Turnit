Recap · 10 / 11

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓The stock market's core idea — trading ownership pieces of companies — dates back to 1602.
✓A quote shows a symbol, a price, and how much it's changed — nothing more mysterious than that.
✓Every trade is just a buyer's bid meeting a seller's ask at an agreed price.
✓Exchanges like the NYSE and Nasdaq are where these trades actually happen, on a set schedule.
✓Bull means prices are broadly rising. Bear means they're broadly falling.
✓Indices like the S&P 500 measure the market's health — they're gauges, not something you buy directly.
Your next move: next time you hear "the market was up today," you'll know exactly what that actually means.
