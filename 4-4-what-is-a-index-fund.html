Flashcards: Myth or Fact · 9 / 11

Flip each to check yourself

Tap Myth or Fact to flip the card and see the real answer.

An index fund and "the index" are literally the same thing.

MYTH

The index is a formula. The index fund is the purchasable product built to track it.

Index funds are passively managed, with no one picking stocks to try to beat the market.

FACT

It mechanically mirrors the index instead of relying on a manager's stock picks.

Every fund, including index funds, charges some kind of expense ratio.

FACT

Index funds just tend to charge far less than actively managed ones.

Because index funds are diversified, they can never lose value.

MYTH

If the whole market drops, an index fund drops right along with it.

Recap · 10 / 11

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓The index is a formula. The index fund is the real, purchasable product built to track it.
✓An index fund holds the same companies as the index, in the same weighted proportions.
✓The biggest companies can make up a huge share of the fund — the weighting isn't equal.
✓Passive index funds mechanically mirror the market instead of trying to beat it.
✓Lower expense ratios mean index funds tend to keep more of your return over time.
✓Diversification softens one company failing — it can't soften the whole market falling.
