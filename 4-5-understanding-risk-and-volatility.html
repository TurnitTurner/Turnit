Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Beta shows how much a stock swings compared to the market's own 1.0 baseline.
✓The VIX tracks how much volatility investors expect — the market's own fear gauge.
✓Correlation shows whether two investments tend to move together, oppositely, or independently.
✓Some sectors, like early-stage tech, tend to run hotter than others, like utilities.
✓Risk capacity is what your timeline can afford. Risk tolerance is what your gut can handle.
✓These tools describe risk more precisely — they don't replace the fundamentals from Module 1.
