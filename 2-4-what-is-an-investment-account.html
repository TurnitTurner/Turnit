Recap · 11 / 12

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓An investing account is just the container. Opening one isn't the same as investing yet.
✓What you buy inside it — cash, a stock, or a fund — is what decides whether it actually grows.
✓Some containers, like checking accounts, can't hold investments at all.
✓Money that's actually invested gets time to compound. Money that just sits doesn't.
✓A basket with one thing in it is riskier than one spread across several investments.
✓Automating your contributions is the easiest way to keep the basket filling itself.
Your next move: decide what your first basket would actually hold — then look at the brokerage account in the next lesson.
