Myth or Fact · 8 / 13

Myth or Fact?

Three common beliefs about inflation. Tap your answer for each.

Inflation only happens when the stock market is crashing.

Myth. Standard, mild inflation (roughly 2%) is actually considered a sign of a healthy, functioning economy — it happens in good times and bad.

High-Yield Savings Accounts completely protect your cash from inflation.

Myth. HYSAs help a lot compared to a 0% account, but after taxes, their real yield can still sometimes fall short of a heavy inflation stretch.

Companies with strong pricing power have historically acted as a defense against inflation.

Fact. Businesses that can raise their own prices without losing customers are often able to pass rising costs straight through, protecting their value.

Recap · 12 / 13

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Inflation quietly reduces what the same dollar can buy, even while the number in your account stays the same.
✓Cash sitting still tends to lose real value over time; assets growing faster than inflation tend to gain it.
✓Inflation comes from several sources: demand-pull, cost-push, supply shocks, and money supply growth.
✓A raise isn't real growth unless it outpaces inflation — otherwise it can be a quiet pay cut.
✓Real ways to fight back: inflation-protected bonds, pricing-power stocks, real estate, and diversified funds.
✓Automating your investing is the simplest way to consistently stay ahead of rising prices.
Your next move: check whether your savings are actually outpacing inflation — and automate a fix if they're not.
