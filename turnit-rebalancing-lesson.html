Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Skip rebalancing, and your risk level can quietly climb without you ever deciding to take it on.
✓Calendar-based rebalancing is predictable. Threshold-based reacts to actual drift.
✓Directing new contributions toward the underweighted asset can rebalance without selling anything.
✓Rebalancing is essentially tax-free inside a Roth or Traditional IRA, unlike a taxable brokerage account.
✓Rebalancing can modestly help returns by trimming winners and adding to laggards — but it's not guaranteed.
✓The real point of rebalancing is managing risk, not chasing extra return.
