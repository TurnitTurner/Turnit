Recap · 9 / 10

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓A standard brokerage account has no contribution limit, no age requirement, and no income limit.
✓You deposit cash, buy investments with it, and sell them again whenever you choose.
✓You only owe tax on a gain once you actually sell for a profit — not while you're still holding.
✓Sell within a year and the gain is taxed as ordinary income. Hold past a year and the rate is usually lower.
✓Stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, and bonds all fit inside. A car or a house does not.
✓You can withdraw at any time, for any reason, which is why this account suits money you may need before retirement.
Your next move: set up one automatic contribution, however small — then look at Roth vs. Traditional IRAs in the next lesson.
