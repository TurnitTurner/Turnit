Recap · 8 / 9

Here's what you now know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Losses tend to feel considerably more painful than equivalent gains feel good — that's loss aversion.
✓How often to check depends on what the account is actually for, not a single universal rule.
✓Checking is an emotional glance. Reviewing is a scheduled, deliberate look with a purpose.
✓An annual review means checking allocation drift, goal progress, life changes, and fees.
✓Big life events are worth a review whenever they happen, not just on your regular schedule.
✓Less frequent, more deliberate attention beats frequent, emotional glances.
Your next move: pick an actual date, once a year, and put your first real review on the calendar.
