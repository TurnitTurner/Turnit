Myth or Fact · 8 / 13

Myth or Fact?

Three things people get wrong about investing. Tap your answer for each.

You need a lot of money to start investing.

Myth. Many platforms let you start with just a few dollars using fractional shares — you don't need thousands to begin.

Investing always carries some risk of loss.

Fact. There's no such thing as risk-free growth. Every investment can lose value, which is exactly why timeline and diversification matter.

Investing is basically the same thing as gambling.

Myth. Gambling has a built-in house edge with no expected long-term payoff. Broad, long-term investing is built around companies actually growing over time.

Recap · 12 / 13

Here's What You Now Know

The whole lesson, in six lines.

✓Saving protects money you need soon. Investing grows money you won't touch for years.
✓Time in the market and consistency drive most of your long-term results.
✓Waiting to start doesn't just delay contributions — it gives up years of compounding you never get back.
✓Investing always carries real risk — that's different from gambling, but it's never risk-free.
✓Avoid the traps: hot tips, ignored fees, and putting too much in one holding.
✓Investing consistently — and automatically — beats trying to time it perfectly.
Your next move: build (or check on) a small emergency fund, then automate a first small investment. That's how good habits build themselves.
